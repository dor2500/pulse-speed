**Ready for Google Play**

- **Fix:** the Ping tab could crash on Android 8 and 9 while looking up the router.
- **Delete account:** Settings → Cloud sync → *Delete account* now permanently deletes your account and all synced history. It used to delete only the data.
- **Privacy policy** in Settings → About, and a page explaining how to delete your account.
- Hebrew now works in the Play Store build too when you switch language inside the app.
- Removed two permissions the sign-in library added (fingerprint/biometric) that Pulse doesn't use.
- Android TV boxes connected by cable can now install the app from Play.

**Install:** open Settings → Updates in the app, or download `Pulse-v1.7.1.apk` below.

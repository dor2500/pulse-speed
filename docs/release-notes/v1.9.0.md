**Full test details, service status and two new test networks**

- **Full details for every test:** tap a result in History (or *Full details* after a test) to see data used, ping when idle and during download and upload (low, high and jitter for each), packet loss, connection type and carrier, server, protocol, connections, device, test length, and internal and external IP. IP addresses stay on your phone and are never synced.
- **History:** no more swipe-to-delete. Each result has a delete button with a confirmation.
- **Service status** (new, first in Tools): is WhatsApp, Bezeq, YouTube or Netflix down? 34 services, each checked live from your connection, with official status pages where they exist and reports from Pulse users, including a 24-hour chart. Reporting is anonymous.
- **New test networks:** **Apple** (the CDN behind iPhone and Mac speed checks) and **M-Lab** (Measurement Lab's open research servers, including one in Tel Aviv). M-Lab publishes every test, including your IP address, as open data.
- **Fix:** on slow connections a test could get stuck at the end of the upload. It now always finishes on time.
- **Fix:** a new result could vanish from History if the cloud didn't accept it. It now stays on your phone and is sent again.
- **Fix:** a few sites behind strict firewalls (like gov.il) showed as down in Service status when they weren't.

**Install:** open Settings → Updates in the app, or download `Pulse-v1.9.0.apk` below.

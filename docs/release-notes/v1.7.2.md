**Google sign-in is live**

- **Cloud sync works:** Settings → Cloud sync → *Sign in with Google* backs up your speed test history and keeps it in sync across your devices.
- Fixed the sync status getting stuck on "Offline" after the data had already synced.
- The status now says "Synced just now" instead of "0 minutes ago".

**Install:** open Settings → Updates in the app, or download `Pulse-v1.7.2.apk` below.

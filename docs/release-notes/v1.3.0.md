**Compact navigation and a new History screen**

- **Compact floating nav bar**: a small centred 60dp pill that hugs its icons and floats clear of the gesture bar, with a smaller tonal indicator behind the selected tab.
- **History summary card** with asymmetric corners and a mini download-trend sparkline, plus average, best and ping.
- **Filter chips**: All, Wi‑Fi, Cellular.
- **Redesigned history cards**: the download speed is the large headline number; upload, ping and location sit in compact tonal labels.
- Hebrew: number-and-unit labels now read correctly ("100 ms").

**Install:** if you have v1.2.0, open Settings → Updates in the app. Otherwise download `Pulse-v1.3.0.apk` below; it updates in place and keeps your history.
